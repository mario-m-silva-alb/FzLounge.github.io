About FzLounge

Our story, our values, and the community that makes it all happen.

Our Story

FzLounge was born from a simple idea: Trading Card Games are more fun when shared. A small group of friends who met at a local card shop realised that many players had duplicate cards gathering dust while others were searching for the exact same cards online.

In 2023 we formalized FzLounge as a non-profit association, dedicated to connecting collectors, casual players, and competitive duelists in a single, trusted community. No profit is made — any contributions go directly into running the platform and organising community events.

Today, FzLounge supports Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon TCG, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Disney Lorcana, Riftbound, Grand Archive, Digimon TCG, Gundam TCG, and One Piece TCG — with more games being added as our community grows.

Mission Statement

"To create a welcoming, transparent, and community-driven space where every TCG player — regardless of budget or experience — can discover, share, and enjoy Trading Card Game products with fellow enthusiasts."
