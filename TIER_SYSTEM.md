## 📏 Rules & Policies

### Eligibility Requirements

**3-Month Rule:**
- Benefits require **at least 3 consecutive months** of valid membership
- This rule prevents abuse and ensures fair access
- Applies to all tier benefits including discounts, credits, and priority

**Purpose:**
- Protects against membership manipulation
- Ensures commitment to the community
- Maintains fair benefit distribution

---

### Upgrading Tiers

**Process:**
1. Choose your new tier
2. Pay the **price difference** for the last 3 months
3. Wait for the 3-month period to complete
4. Benefits from the new tier activate after payment period

**Example:**
- Currently: Bronze (€10/month)
- Upgrade to: Gold (€30/month)
- Difference: €20/month × 3 months = **€60**
- Pay €60 upfront, continue paying €30/month
- Gold benefits activate after 3 months

**Important:**
- No benefits during the 3-month upgrade period
- Only the price difference is required (not full amount)
- After 3 months, all new tier benefits are available

---

### Priority System

**How It Works:**
- Higher tier members have **priority** in:
  - Event registrations
  - Preorder fulfillment
  - Limited stock situations
  - Table reservations

**Priority Order:**
1. Platinum (Highest)
2. Gold
3. Silver
4. Bronze
5. Wood (Standard)

**Example Scenario:**
```
Available Stock: 2 booster boxes
Orders:
- Wood member: 1 box (ordered first)
- Bronze member: 2 boxes (ordered second)

Result:
- Bronze member receives 2 boxes (priority)
- Wood member waits for restock
```

**Why?**
- Rewards those who support the association
- Ensures community sustainability
- Fair system based on contribution level

---

### Credit System

**What Is Credit?**
- Monthly monetary credit for use within the association
- Accumulates each month based on tier
- Use it or lose it (does not roll over)

**Where Can You Use Credit?**
- ✅ Tournament entry fees
- ✅ Bar items (drinks, snacks)
- ❌ **NOT** for materials (sleeves, dice, playmats)

**Credit by Tier:**
- Wood: €0/month
- Bronze: €6/month
- Silver: €11/month
- Gold: €15/month
- Platinum: €15/month

**Important:**
- Credit resets monthly
- Cannot be transferred between members
- Cannot be converted to cash

---

### Materials & Accessories Policy

**Materials Include:**
- Card sleeves
- Dice
- Playmats
- Deckboxes
- Binders
- Other gaming accessories

**Rules:**
1. **Credit cannot be used** for materials
2. **Tier discounts do not apply** to materials
3. **10% markup** when purchasing materials (minimum €1 increase)

**Why?**
- Materials have different profit margins
- Helps maintain inventory
- Separates consumables from gaming products

---

### Abuse Policy

**Zero Tolerance:**
Any abuse of tier benefits will result in:
1. Immediate tier revocation
2. Loss of all tier benefits
3. **Permanent ban from the association**
4. No refunds

**What Counts as Abuse?**
- Selling pre-release products before official release
- Sharing or transferring tier benefits
- Creating multiple accounts for benefits
- Manipulating the priority system
- Fraudulent payment or chargebacks
- Reselling products obtained with member discounts at markup

**We Take This Seriously:**
- The tier system relies on trust
- Abuse hurts all members
- Protects the integrity of the community
- One strike = permanent ban

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### Maximize Your Benefits

**Bronze/Silver Members:**
- Use your credit for tournaments (better value than bar)
- Preorder products to secure allocation
- Attend weekly events to use full credit

**Gold/Platinum Members:**
- Buy singles regularly to benefit from 5% discount
- Use table reservation for Commander nights
- Preorder every new set to maximize discounts
- Platinum: Plan prerelease attendance every 2 months

### Break-Even Analysis

**Bronze Tier (€10/month):**
- €6 credit = 60% value back
- Need €1 discount on 1 product per month = €12 value
- **Breaks even** if you buy 1 product >€10 per month

**Silver Tier (€20/month):**
- €11 credit = 55% value back
- Early pickup = valuable for resellers/collectors
- **Breaks even** if you buy 2 products >€10 per month

**Gold Tier (€30/month):**
- €15 credit = 50% value back
- €2 discount on 8 products/month = €16 saved
- 5% on €100 singles = €5 saved
- Free table (€10 value)
- **Breaks even** if you spend €100+ monthly

**Platinum Tier (€40/month):**
- €15 credit + prerelease (€12.50/month avg) = €27.50 value
- €2 discount on products
- Highest priority (invaluable during high demand)
- **Breaks even** if you attend prereleases and buy sealed products

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I pause my membership?
**A:** No pausing. You can cancel and rejoin, but the 3-month eligibility resets.

### Q: Do I get a refund if I cancel?
**A:** No refunds for partial months. Pay monthly for flexibility.

### Q: Can I share my membership with family?
**A:** No. Memberships are individual and non-transferable.

### Q: What happens if I miss a payment?
**A:** Membership pauses after 7 days grace period. Reactivate by paying outstanding balance.

### Q: Can I upgrade mid-month?
**A:** Yes, pay the difference for remaining days + 3-month upgrade fee.

### Q: Is the 3-month rule strict?
**A:** Yes. No exceptions. This protects all members.

### Q: Can I use credit from multiple months?
**A:** No. Credit expires monthly and doesn't accumulate.

### Q: Do discounts apply to already discounted items?
**A:** No. Tier discounts don't stack with sale/clearance prices.

### Q: Can I get Platinum benefits immediately?
**A:** No. Must complete 3 consecutive months first (unless upgrading from Gold).

### Q: What if I abuse benefits accidentally?
**A:** Contact us immediately. Honest mistakes may be forgiven; intentional abuse = ban.
