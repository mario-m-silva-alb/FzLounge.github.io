Important Information

✓

Eligibility Requirements

To enjoy tier benefits, you must have at least 3 consecutive months of valid membership. This rule prevents abuse and ensures fair access to all members.

⬆️

Upgrading Your Tier

Want to upgrade? You'll need to pay the price difference for the last 3 months. Benefits from the new tier will be available after this payment period.

🏆

Priority System

Higher tier members have priority in event registrations and preorders. This rewards those who support the association and community.

Example: If only 2 boxes are available, a Bronze member who ordered 2 boxes will receive their full order before a Wood member who ordered 1 box.
⚠️

Abuse Policy

Any abuse of tier benefits may result in tier revocation and association ban. We take this seriously to protect all members.

💳

Credit Usage

Monthly credit can be used for tournaments and bar items only. Credit cannot be applied to materials (sleeves, dice, etc.).

🛡️

Materials & Accessories

Materials like sleeves have a 10% markup (minimum €1) when purchased with credit. Tier discounts do not apply to these items.
